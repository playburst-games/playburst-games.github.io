How to play
1Drag anywhere to move. Your gun fires on its own. Pillars block every bullet.
2DASH through bullets. Kills charge OVERDRIVE: tap it to slow time.
3Pick a power-up after every wave. Max two matching powers to evolve a weapon.
4Gold-crowned elites drop hearts or power orbs. Grab treasure chests before they vanish.
!A different boss every 5th wave. Each boss makes the next level tougher.

Laptop: WASD or arrows to move, Shift or Space to dash, E for Overdrive, P to pause.
